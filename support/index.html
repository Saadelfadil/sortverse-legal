Use the subject “SORTVERSE bug”, and tell us:

  • your phone model and Android or iOS version;

Gameplay help

How do I move balls?

Tap a tube to pick up the balls on top, then tap another tube to drop them. You move the run of same-colored balls from the top, as many as fit. A ball can go into an empty tube or onto a ball of the same color. Fill every tube with a single color to win.

I made a mistake. Can I go back?

Yes. Undo and Restart are always free, as often as you like.

What happens if I get stuck?

If a puzzle can no longer be solved from where you are, SORTVERSE tells you and suggests a way back, such as undoing a few moves or restarting.

How do hints work?

A hint shows a good next move. Hints cost Sort Coins, which you earn by clearing levels. You can also use a free hint you've earned, or choose to watch a short optional video for one.

Why can't I use a tube in Crystal Caverns?

It's frozen. A frozen tube can't be used until it thaws. Its badge shows how many tubes you need to finish first, and it counts down as you sort.

How do Candy Comet's containers work?
  • Candy Cup: holds 2 balls of any color, never counts as finished, and must be empty when you solve the level.
  • Flavour Jar: accepts only its own flavour (color).
  • Sugar Funnel: candy goes in, but never comes back out. Undo can still take a drop back.
Do I need the internet to play?

No. Every level works offline. You only need a connection for the optional Arena and optional videos.

Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?

Your progress is saved on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it, unless your phone's own backup restores it. Install updates over the existing app; don't uninstall first.
