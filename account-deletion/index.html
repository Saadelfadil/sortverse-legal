Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your SORTVERSE Arena profile and anonymous online identity.

Draft for owner review: not yet in effect. This notice is removed on publication.

This page is for the SORTVERSE app, published by Saad El Fadil, an individual developer in Morocco. SORTVERSE has no email or password accounts. The only online data is the optional Arena, which exists only if you chose to join it. The Arena is not yet open to the public.

Delete your Arena profile in the app

  1. Open SORTVERSE.
  2. On the home screen, tap Arena.
  3. Tap Arena settings (the settings icon at the top of the Arena screen).
  4. Scroll to Leave the Arena and tap Leave & delete online profile.
  5. In Leave the Arena?, tap Leave & delete to confirm.
  6. Wait for the confirmation: “Arena profile deleted. Your online profile and anonymous identity are gone; your game is unchanged.”

If you're offline

You can start the deletion without a connection. The app shows “You're offline. Deletion finishes when you're back online.”

  • Your progress stops being shared immediately; nothing is uploaded while the deletion is pending.
  • The online deletion completes automatically once you have an internet connection, the next time SORTVERSE starts, comes back to the screen, or you open the Arena.
  • You can't rejoin the Arena until the deletion has finished.
  • If you delete the app before it reconnects, the deletion can't finish from the app. Contact us instead (see below).

What is deleted

  • Your public Arena profile: Arena name, avatar, highest level, world badges and the time you reached that level.
  • Name reports you made, and reports other players made about your name.
  • Your anonymous online identity, including its sign-in sessions. It can't be restored.

The deletion takes effect on our servers as soon as it completes. Short-lived technical logs and any backups kept by our hosting provider, Supabase, expire on their own schedule (see the Privacy Policy).

What stays on your phone

Leaving the Arena doesn't touch your game. Your progress, coins, hints, nickname, avatar and settings stay on your device, and you can keep playing offline.

To delete that local data too, uninstall SORTVERSE. On Android you can also clear it in Settings → Apps → SORTVERSE → Storage → Clear storage; the exact path varies by device. Note that uninstalling alone does not delete an Arena profile: leave the Arena first.

If you can't use the app

If you've deleted the app, changed phones or can't open the Arena, email us. There is no automated web deletion form.

saadelfadil18@gmail.com

Please use the subject “SORTVERSE Arena deletion request” and include:

  • your exact Arena name;
  • your avatar and approximate highest level;
  • roughly when you joined or last played;
  • your device type (Android or iPhone);
  • if you still can, a screenshot of your Arena screen showing your name.

Verification

  • Arena profiles are anonymous and their names are public. To stop someone deleting another player's profile, we check the details you send against our records, and we may ask follow-up questions.
  • We will never ask for a password or payment.
  • If we can't confirm that a profile is yours, we won't delete or hide it. We'll reply to explain what's missing and what else you can send.
  • Every request is reviewed by a person; nothing is deleted automatically from an email.

We aim to acknowledge your request within 7 days, and to complete it within the time limits set by the law that applies to your request.

Ads and consent data

SORTVERSE doesn't keep ad data itself. Optional rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob.

  • Consent choices: where they apply, you can change them in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device settings.
  • Data Google holds: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy. Questions? Visit Support.
