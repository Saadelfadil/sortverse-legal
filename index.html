How to play

Three steps. Endless “just one more”.

Easy to learn, satisfying to master. No timers, no pressure.

  1. 1

    Pick a Tube

    Choose the balls you want to move.

  2. 2

    Sort the Colors

    Move matching colors into the right containers.

  3. 3

    Explore New Worlds

    Complete puzzles, earn rewards, and unlock new challenges.

Features

Made to feel good

Relaxing puzzles, friendly help when you need it, and an optional community.

  • 75 levels, three worlds

    25 puzzles in each world, from gentle warm-ups to real brain-teasers.

  • Soft Landing

    Balls glide and settle smoothly into place with every move.

  • Play offline

    Every puzzle works without an internet connection.

  • Smart Stuck Detection

    If a puzzle can no longer be solved, SORTVERSE lets you know and shows you a way back.

  • Free Undo and Restart

    Take back any move or start over, as often as you like, at no cost.

  • Sort Coins and hints

    Earn coins by clearing levels and spend them on hints when a puzzle gets tough. Coins have no cash value.

  • SORTVERSE Arena Coming later

    An optional community where you can share your progress, only if you choose to join. It opens once its safety and moderation features are ready.

  • Anonymous by design

    In the Arena, no email or password. Join with a generated Arena name (or one you choose) and your avatar, and leave whenever you want.

  • Casual global leaderboard

    See the top 100 Arena players by highest level reached. Self-reported and just for fun.

  • Android and iOS

    Built for phones on both platforms.
