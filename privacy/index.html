3. Information stored on your device

SORTVERSE saves the following on your phone so you can keep playing, including offline:

  • your nickname and chosen avatar;
  • game progress: levels completed, best results and attempts;
  • Sort Coins, free hints and a short history of coin rewards and spending;
  • how many reward videos you've watched today, so daily limits apply;
  • settings such as sound and haptics;
  • a backup copy of your save, used to recover it if the main copy is damaged;
  • if you join the Arena: your Arena state and the sign-in session for your anonymous Arena identity;
  • if you are asked for ad consent: the choices you make, which Google's consent tool stores on your device.

This information is not sent to us. Your nickname is not published unless you choose it as your Arena name. Deleting the app (or clearing its data in your phone's settings) deletes this information from the device. Your phone's own backup features, such as Android backup or iCloud, may include app data in backups held by Google or Apple under your account and their terms.

4. SORTVERSE Arena (optional)

The Arena is an optional, casual community leaderboard. It is not yet open to the public. We will only offer it once its anti-abuse and moderation safeguards have been verified. This section describes how it works when it is available.

Nothing is sent to the Arena unless you tap Join Arena, and you can play every level without it.

What joining creates

  • An anonymous online identity. No email, phone number or password. Our server provider, Supabase, creates a random user ID and a sign-in session for your app.
  • A public Arena profile, which appears once you reach level 5. It contains:
    • your Arena name: a generated name by default, or your nickname or a name you type, if it passes our name filter;
    • your avatar;
    • your highest level reached and the world badges that follow from it;
    • the time you reached that level, used to break ties.

Other players who have joined the Arena can see the public profile on the leaderboard (top 100). Arena progress is self-reported by the app and isn't verified.

Information that isn't public

  • The time your profile was created and last updated (used for rate limits).
  • Name reports: if you report another player's name, we store who reported whom and when, to review it. Reports are never shown to other players.
  • A moderation flag: we may hide a profile with an offensive name.
  • Technical data: when the app talks to the Arena, Supabase processes connection data such as your IP address and request details to provide, secure and rate-limit the service.

Your saved game, coins, rewards, hints, attempts, settings and ad activity are never sent to the Arena.

If you reinstall the app or move to a new phone, the old anonymous identity can't be recovered from the app. Its profile stays until it's deleted (see section 10).

5. Ads and consent (Google AdMob)

SORTVERSE offers optional rewarded video ads: for example, a video for bonus Sort Coins, a free hint or a doubled level reward. A video plays only when you tap a clearly labelled button. There are no banner, pop-up or app-open ads.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob.

  • Consent check at launch: each time the app starts, Google's consent tool contacts Google to check whether consent is required where you are.
  • Background loading: where ads may be requested, the app loads one video in the background so it's ready when you want it. A video still plays only when you tap.

When the app requests or shows an ad, Google's software collects information to deliver, measure and protect ads. According to Google's disclosures, this can include:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • device identifiers. On Android, this includes the advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent choices

  • In regions where consent is required (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app uses Google's consent tool to ask for your choices before any ad is requested. You'll see it the first time you choose to watch a video.
  • In those regions, the app does not request ads from Google until you've made your choices. After that, Google decides which ads, if any, it can show based on your choices and its policies. For example, if you don't consent, it may show only limited or non-personalised ads, or none. The rest of the game is never affected.
  • When it applies, you can change your choices at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy options.

Tracking and advertising identifiers

  • iPhone: SORTVERSE does not ask for permission to track you through Apple's App Tracking Transparency, so it cannot access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (for example Settings → Privacy → Ads; the exact path varies by device).

Ads in SORTVERSE are limited to content Google rates as suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG) or below.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't ask for your email, phone number, real name, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services in the app.
  • We don't offer in-app purchases. Sort Coins can't be bought and have no cash value.

9. How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you delete it, or delete or reset the app.
  • Arena profile and anonymous identity: until you delete them (see section 10). Inactive Arena profiles are not deleted automatically.
  • Name reports: kept until the reporting or reported profile is deleted.
  • Server logs: kept by Supabase for a short period set by its service plan, then deleted automatically.
  • Backups: we don't make our own backups of Arena data. If the hosting plan used for the public Arena keeps backups, we'll state how long here before the Arena opens.
  • Support emails: kept in our mailbox until we delete them; there is no fixed period yet.
  • Ad data: held by Google under Google's retention policies.

If we introduce automatic deletion or fixed retention periods, we'll update this policy before they take effect.

10. Deleting your data

  • Arena: in the app, open Arena → Arena settings → Leave & delete online profile. This deletes your public profile, reports you made and reports about you, and your anonymous identity with its sessions. Full instructions, including what to do if you no longer have the app, are on the Account & Data Deletion page.
  • Game data on your device: delete the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings.
  • Ad consent: change it at any time in Settings → Privacy options (shown where it applies). For data Google holds, see Google's information.

11. Children

SORTVERSE is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

The game doesn't ask for personal information to play, and joining the Arena doesn't require an email or real name. If you believe a child has joined the Arena and you would like their profile removed, contact us at saadelfadil18@gmail.com.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access, correct or delete your personal information;
  • object to or restrict its processing;
  • receive a copy of it;
  • withdraw consent at any time.

You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. In Morocco, that's the CNDP (Commission nationale de contrôle de la protection des données à caractère personnel).

To make a request, email saadelfadil18@gmail.com. Because the Arena is anonymous, we can only find your profile from details you give us, such as your exact Arena name. We may ask for reasonable proof that the profile is yours before deleting or changing it.

We aim to acknowledge every privacy request within 7 days, and to complete it within the time limits set by the law that applies to your request.

13. International processing

  • Arena: the development version of the Arena is hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). The public Arena has not been set up yet; we'll state where it's hosted here before it opens.
  • Google and Vercel: both may process data in the United States and other countries, under the safeguards described in their policies.

14. Security

Connections between the app and the Arena are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to Arena data is restricted by database rules so that players can only read public profile fields and can only change their own profile. The app contains no secret server keys. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect the information we handle.

15. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising. It's hosted by Vercel, which processes technical request information, such as your IP address, when you visit, as described in its privacy notice. Links to email open your own mail app.
