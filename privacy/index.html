1. Who we are

SORTVERSE is a puzzle game for iPhone and Android. This policy covers both versions of the app and this website. Both apps offer the same game, the same optional rewarded videos and the same optional online Arena. Where they differ, for example in how the app is checked when you join the Arena or how advertising identifiers work, this policy says so.

  • Developer and data controller: Saad El Fadil, an individual developer
  • Country: Morocco
  • Privacy and deletion requests: privacy@sortverse.site
  • Support: support@sortverse.site
  • General inquiries: contact@sortverse.site

In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the developer named above.

3. Information stored on your device

SORTVERSE saves the following on your phone so you can keep playing, including offline:

  • your nickname and chosen avatar;
  • game progress: levels completed, best results and attempts;
  • Sort Coins, free hints and a short history of coin rewards and spending;
  • how many reward videos you've watched today, so daily limits apply;
  • settings such as sound and haptics;
  • a backup copy of your save, used to recover it if the main copy is damaged;
  • if you join the Arena: your Arena state and the sign-in session for your anonymous Arena identity;
  • if you are asked for ad consent: the choices you make, which Google's consent tool stores on your device.

This information is not sent to us. Your nickname is not published unless you choose it as your Arena name. Deleting the app (or clearing its data in your phone's settings) deletes this information from the device. Your phone's own backup features, such as iCloud Backup on iPhone or Google's backup on Android, may include app data in backups held by Apple or Google under your account and its terms.

4. SORTVERSE Arena (optional)

The Arena is an optional, casual community leaderboard, offered in both the iPhone and Android apps. Players on both share one leaderboard. Everything in this section applies to both apps, except how the app is checked when you join (App Attest on iPhone, Google Play Integrity on Android; see below). We open it in stages and only after its anti-abuse and moderation safeguards have been verified (see “How availability is controlled” below).

Nothing is sent to the Arena unless you tap Join Arena, and you can play every level without it.

What joining creates

  • An anonymous online identity. No email, phone number or password. Our server provider, Supabase, creates a random user ID and a sign-in session for your app.
  • A public Arena profile, which appears once you reach level 5. It contains:
    • your Arena name: a generated name by default, or your nickname or a name you type, if it passes our name filter;
    • your avatar;
    • your highest level reached and the world badges that follow from it;
    • the time you reached that level, used to break ties.

Checking that the app is genuine (iPhone)

To keep automated fake accounts out of the Arena, joining uses Apple's App Attest service. Your iPhone creates a key in its secure hardware, and Apple certifies that the request comes from a genuine copy of SORTVERSE. Our server checks Apple's certificate and gives the app a single-use join ticket. We keep only the random single-use challenge and a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the ticket, and delete them about a day after they expire. We don't store the key, Apple's certificate or any device identifier. Apple's part is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Checking that the app is genuine (Android)

On Android, joining uses Google's Play Integrity service for the same purpose. The app asks Google Play for an integrity verdict tied to a random single-use challenge from our server. Google checks that the request comes from a genuine copy of SORTVERSE installed from Google Play, on a device that passes Google's integrity checks, and returns an encrypted verdict. Our server asks Google to decrypt the verdict, checks it, and gives the app a single-use join ticket. As on iPhone, we keep only the random single-use challenge and a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the ticket, and delete them about a day after they expire. We don't store the verdict or any device identifier, and we don't log them. If Google's checks fail, for example on some modified or emulated devices, you can't join the Arena; the rest of the game works normally. Google's part is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Other players who have joined the Arena can see the public profile on the leaderboard (top 100). Arena progress is self-reported by the app and isn't verified.

Information that isn't public

  • The time your profile was created and last updated (used for rate limits).
  • Name reports: if you report another player's name, we store who reported whom and when, to review it. Reports are never shown to other players.
  • Moderation: reported names are reviewed by the developer, by hand. We may hide a profile with an offensive name, and can restore it. Reports never hide a profile automatically. Each moderation action, such as hiding or restoring a profile, is recorded with the profile's anonymous ID, the time and a short note.
  • Sign-in session: Supabase's sign-in service stores your anonymous identity's session together with the IP address and the technical description of the app (user agent) used to sign in. It is deleted with your anonymous identity.
  • Technical data: when the app talks to the Arena, Supabase processes connection data such as your IP address and request details to provide, secure and rate-limit the service. For rate limits, we turn your IP address into a one-way code using a secret that changes every day, so codes can't be linked across days; the codes are deleted after 2 days.

Your saved game, coins, rewards, hints, attempts, settings and ad activity are never sent to the Arena.

How availability is controlled

Arena availability is controlled by a server-side configuration that can be changed without an app update. Access moves through defined stages — from internal testing to limited release to public — and includes an emergency shutdown option that can pause the Arena in response to a safety or operational incident. The app checks this configuration before allowing any write to the leaderboard. If the Arena is paused or not yet available in your stage, the rest of the game is never affected.

If you reinstall the app or move to a new phone, the old anonymous identity can't be recovered from the app. Its profile stays until it's deleted (see section 10).

5. Ads and consent (Google AdMob)

SORTVERSE offers optional rewarded video ads: for example, a short video for a hint. A video plays only when you tap a clearly labelled button. There are no banner, pop-up or app-open ads.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob.

  • Consent check at launch: each time the app starts, Google's consent tool contacts Google to check whether consent is required where you are and, where it is, whether you've already made your choices. This sends technical information, such as your IP address, to Google. The app doesn't request any ad unless this check has succeeded, so if it can't complete (for example, when you're offline), no ad is requested.
  • When ads may be requested: Google's ad software is started, and ads are requested, only once the consent tool indicates that ads may be requested: straight away where consent isn't required, or, where consent is required, only after you've made your choices and Google's consent tool then allows ad requests (see “Your consent choices” below).
  • Background loading: once ads may be requested, the app loads one video in the background when it starts, so it's ready when you want it. A video still plays only when you tap. Because of this, Google's ad software collects the information below even if you never watch a video. You can't switch this background loading off inside the app, but your consent choices and device settings limit how Google may use the information (see below).

When the app requests or shows an ad, Google's software collects information to deliver, measure and protect ads. According to Google's disclosures, this can include:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; on Android, unless you've deleted it; see below), or identifiers limited to one app or one developer, which Google says may be used for advertising and analytics.

This information is shared with Google. Google's ad software in the app sends it directly to Google. Google is not acting only on our behalf here: according to Google's disclosures, it uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, including for its own purposes. App-store privacy summaries describe this as data “shared” with a third party. We don't receive individual players' data from Google through AdMob's reporting: AdMob gives us aggregated reports about ad performance, such as how many ads were shown and the revenue they earned. We don't use a separate analytics service in the app.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent choices

  • In regions where consent is required (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app uses Google's consent tool to ask for your choices before any ad is requested. You'll see it the first time you choose to watch a video.
  • In those regions, the app does not request ads from Google until you've made your choices. After that, Google decides which ads, if any, it can show based on your choices and its policies. For example, if you don't consent, it may show only limited or non-personalised ads, or none. The rest of the game is never affected.
  • When it applies, you can change your choices at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy options.

Tracking and advertising identifiers on iPhone

  • Apple's tracking permission: the first time you tap an optional video, and only after any consent choices required where you are, iOS asks whether SORTVERSE may track you (App Tracking Transparency). It asks once.
  • If you tap Allow: Google's ad software may use your iPhone's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show you more relevant ads and to measure them, which can involve data from other companies' apps and websites.
  • If you tap Ask App Not to Track, or tracking is restricted on your iPhone: iOS doesn't give the identifier to the app or to Google. Videos and rewards work the same either way.
  • You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Even without the IDFA, Google's software can use identifiers limited to one app or one developer to deliver, measure and protect ads, as described in Google's disclosures.

Advertising identifiers on Android

  • No tracking prompt: Android has no equivalent of Apple's tracking permission, and the app doesn't show one.
  • Advertising ID: Google's ad software may use your device's advertising ID to show ads, including personalised ads where your consent choices and Google's policies allow, and to measure them. This can involve data from other companies' apps and websites.
  • Your control: you can delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (on most phones, Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). Once it's deleted, apps can no longer use it for ads. Videos and rewards work the same either way.
  • Google's software can also use an app set ID, limited to apps from the same developer, and other identifiers limited to one app, to deliver, measure and protect ads, as described in Google's disclosures.

Ads in SORTVERSE are limited to content Google rates as suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG) or below.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information for money.
  • If you allow tracking on iPhone, or keep your advertising ID on Android (see section 5), Google may use your data for personalised advertising across other companies' apps and websites. Some US state laws call this “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can stop it at any time: on iPhone, choose Ask App Not to Track or use Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Where consent applies, you can also change your choices in Settings → Privacy options in the app.
  • We don't ask for your email, phone number, real name, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services in the app. (Google's ad software collects its own diagnostic data; see section 5.)
  • We don't offer in-app purchases. Sort Coins can't be bought and have no cash value.

9. How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you delete it, or delete or reset the app.
  • Arena (iPhone and Android): the periods below apply to both apps.
  • Arena profile and anonymous identity: until you delete them (see section 10), or automatically once the profile hasn't been updated for 12 months.
  • Anonymous identity without a public profile (you joined but never reached level 5): deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Name reports: deleted 90 days after we've resolved them, or earlier when the reporting or reported profile is deleted.
  • Moderation records (such as hiding a name): 12 months.
  • Join challenges and ticket fingerprints (App Attest on iPhone, Play Integrity on Android): about a day after they expire. Play Integrity verdicts aren't stored. Rate-limit codes: 2 days.
  • Exceptions: we may keep specific Arena records longer while we investigate abuse or a safety report, or where the law requires it, and delete them once that's finished.
  • Sign-in sessions: deleted together with your anonymous identity.
  • Server logs: kept by Supabase for a short period set by its service plan, then deleted automatically.
  • Backups: we don't currently make our own backups of Arena data. If we start, we'll update this policy first.
  • Support emails: kept in our mailbox until we delete them; there is no fixed period yet.
  • Ad data: held by Google under Google's retention policies.

If we change these periods, we'll update this policy before the change takes effect.

10. Deleting your data

  • Arena (iPhone and Android): in the app, open Arena → Arena settings → Leave & delete online profile. This deletes your public profile, reports you made and reports about you, and your anonymous identity with its sessions. Full instructions are on the Account & Data Deletion page.
  • If you can't use the app (for example, you deleted it or changed phones), on either platform: email privacy@sortverse.site with the subject “SORTVERSE Arena deletion request”. The Account & Data Deletion page lists the details to include and how we verify requests.
  • Game data on your device: delete the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings.
  • Advertising identifiers: on iPhone, change the tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Ad consent: change it at any time in Settings → Privacy options (shown where it applies). For data Google holds, see Google's information.

11. Children

SORTVERSE is intended for a general audience of teenagers and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

Ads in SORTVERSE are limited to content Google rates PG or below. The Arena needs no email or real name, and names are filtered and can be reported.

The app doesn't ask for your age. If you are below the age at which you can agree to online data processing on your own where you live (for example, under 16 in some EU countries or under 13 in the UK), please ask a parent or guardian before agreeing to personalised ads or allowing tracking. You can always refuse both: videos and rewards work the same either way.

The game doesn't ask for personal information to play, and joining the Arena doesn't require an email or real name. If you believe a child has joined the Arena and you would like their profile removed, contact us at privacy@sortverse.site.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access, correct or delete your personal information;
  • object to or restrict its processing;
  • receive a copy of it;
  • withdraw consent at any time.

Under Morocco's Law No. 09-08, you have the right to access and correct your personal data and, for legitimate reasons, to object to its processing.

You may also complain to a data protection authority. In Morocco, that's the CNDP (Commission nationale de contrôle de la protection des données à caractère personnel). In the EU or the UK, you can complain to the authority where you live or work. In the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

To make a request, email privacy@sortverse.site. Because the Arena is anonymous, we can only find your profile from details you give us, such as your exact Arena name. We may ask for reasonable proof that the profile is yours before deleting or changing it.

We aim to acknowledge every privacy request within 7 days, and to complete it within the time limits set by the law that applies to your request.

13. International processing

  • Arena (iPhone and Android): stored by Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Supabase, Inc. is based in the United States; its privacy policy describes how it handles data.
  • The developer: we operate and moderate the Arena from Morocco, so Arena data is accessed from Morocco.
  • Apple (App Attest, iPhone only): may process data in the United States and other countries, under the safeguards described in its policy.
  • Google (AdMob, its consent tool, Play Integrity and Google Play), Vercel and Namecheap: each may process data in the United States and other countries, under the safeguards described in their policies.

14. Security

Connections between the app and the Arena are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to Arena data is restricted by database rules so that players can only read public profile fields and can only change their own profile. The app contains no secret server keys. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect the information we handle.

15. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising. It's hosted by Vercel, which processes technical request information, such as your IP address, when you visit, as described in its privacy notice. Links to email open your own mail app.

16. Changes and contact
